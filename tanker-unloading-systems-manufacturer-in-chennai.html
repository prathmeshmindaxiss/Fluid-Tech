What Are Tanker Unloading Systems?

A Tanker Unloading System is an integrated fluid transfer and measurement system used to unload liquid products from road tankers into underground or storage tanks. Instead of relying only on manual estimation or basic transfer arrangements, the system measures the quantity being unloaded and provides operators with clear information about flow and total quantity.

The Fluid-Tech system is designed to accurately measure and monitor liquid fuels and solvents during unloading. It incorporates a high-capacity air separator and control valve to help eliminate air from the liquid stream, while the backlit LCD display allows operators to read important parameters conveniently. RS-485 connectivity is also provided for integration and communication requirements.
