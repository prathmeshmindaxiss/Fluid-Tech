DG Fuel Consumption Monitoring System

DG Fuel Consumption Monitoring System

An advanced oval gear positive displacement flow meter designed specifically for precise diesel tracking. It automatically computes exact net fuel consumption from supply and return measurements, making it perfect for vehicle, DG set, and fleet management integration.

Specifications

  • Size: DN- 4 to DN- 25 BSP(F)
  • Flow Range: 5 – 400 LPH
  • Accuracy: ±0.5% of Reading
  • MOC: Aluminium (ENP) / SS 304 / SS 316
  • Max Press / Temp: 16 Kg/cm² / 80°C
  • Power Supply: 12 V DC (Direct Battery Compatible)
  • Communication: RS485 Modbus RTU & Pulse NPN

Key Benefits

  • Computes Net Fuel Consumption (In/Out)
  • 4-Parameter LCD (In, Out, Use, Stock)
  • SCADA & Fleet Management Ready
  • Weatherproof Industrial Enclosure
  • Flow Unit Adjustment via Keypad
  • Proven Oval Gear Mechanism
