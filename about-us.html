About Fluid-Tech Automation

Engineering Precision in Process & Fluid Automation

Fluid-Tech is an eminent manufacturer, supplier, and system integrator specializing in high-precision Flow Measurement Systems, Automated Dispensing Units, and NAS-Class Oil Purification solutions.

Headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra — with cutting-edge manufacturing facilities at MIDC Bhosari — our engineering division brings together over 20 years of technical expertise to design, calibrate, and execute turnkey process skids for India's leading industrial sectors.

Flow Measurement

Positive Displacement meters, chemical sensors & digital batch totalizers.

Automated Dispensing

Tanker decanting, barrel filling skids & mobile fuel bowser systems.

Oil Purification (NAS)

High-efficiency hydraulic oil filtration & particle contamination control.

Custom Skid Integration

Turnkey PLC/SCADA automation panels and bespoke fluid machinery.
