What Is a Preset Batching System?

A Preset Batching System is an automated liquid dispensing and measurement system that allows an operator to set a required quantity before starting the batch. The system measures the liquid flowing through a positive displacement flow meter and controls the dispensing process to achieve the selected quantity.

Fluid-Tech's Pre-Set Batching System uses a time-tested vane-type design and is intended for applications where the same quantity of liquid needs to be supplied repeatedly. According to the manufacturer's product information, the system can provide accuracy of approximately ±0.25%, with available flow ranges depending on the selected meter size.

The basic operating principle is straightforward:

Set Quantity → Start Batch → Measure Flow → Control Valve → Stop at Preset Quantity

This approach can significantly reduce dependency on manual measurement and help maintain repeatable batch quantities during regular production operations.

Applications of Preset Batching Systems

Preset Batching Systems are useful wherever a defined quantity of liquid has to be repeatedly transferred or introduced into a process.

1. Chemical Industries

Chemical manufacturing often requires controlled quantities of liquids to be transferred into reactors, vessels, or process lines. A preset batching arrangement can help maintain consistency and reduce the possibility of quantity-related errors.

2. Oil and Lubricant Industries

Oils and lubricants may need to be dispensed in predetermined quantities during manufacturing, filling, blending, or process operations. Accurate flow measurement can help improve control over material usage.

3. Fuel Handling

Preset batching can be useful for controlled fuel transfer and dispensing applications where a predefined volume needs to be delivered accurately.

4. Process Industries

Manufacturing processes that repeatedly require the same quantity of liquid can benefit from automated batching. The system reduces the need for operators to manually monitor volume during every batch.

5. Storage Tank and Process Vessel Filling

Where a specific quantity must be transferred into a tank or vessel, preset control can help automate the filling cycle and improve repeatability.

6. Industrial Liquid Dispensing

Preset batching can also be applied to general liquid dispensing requirements where controlled quantity, reliable measurement, and repeatable operation are important.

Fluid-Tech specifically describes its batching systems as suitable for precise batching of various chemicals, fuels, and edible oils.
