What Are Pharma PD Flow Meters?

Pharma PD Flow Meters are positive displacement flow measurement devices developed to measure the actual quantity of liquid passing through a pipeline. Unlike flow meters that primarily calculate flow based on velocity, a PD flow meter measures liquid in defined volumes, making it suitable for applications where accurate quantity measurement is important.

FluidTech's Pharma PD Flow Meter uses a single moving-part design and is manufactured using SS 304 / SS 316 material. This makes it suitable for demanding applications involving pharmaceutical liquids, chemicals, and food-processing fluids. The meter is designed for flow ranges from 2 to 400 LPM and supports operating pressure up to 20 kg/cm² with a maximum temperature of 90°C.
