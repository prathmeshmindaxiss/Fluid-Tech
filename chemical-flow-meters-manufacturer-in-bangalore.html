What Are Chemical Flow Meters?

A Chemical Flow Meter is an industrial measuring device used to determine the quantity or flow rate of chemical liquids passing through a pipeline. Depending on the application, different flow-metering technologies can be selected based on fluid characteristics, viscosity, required accuracy, operating conditions, and installation requirements.

FluidTech's Chemical PD Flow Meter uses the positive displacement principle to measure chemical fluids.
