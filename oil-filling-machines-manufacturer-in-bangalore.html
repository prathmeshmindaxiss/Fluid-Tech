What Are Oil Filling Machines?

Oil Filling Machines are industrial equipment used to measure and dispense a predetermined quantity of oil or other liquid products into containers, tanks, drums, equipment, or dispensing systems. Depending on the application, these machines can be used for filling, batching, transferring, and dispensing liquids with controlled flow rates.

Modern oil filling systems commonly use accurate flow sensors and positive displacement technology to maintain consistent measurement. Fluid Tech's range includes PD flow meters and fuel dispensing systems that support accurate liquid measurement across different flow ranges.
