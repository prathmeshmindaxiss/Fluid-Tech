What Are PD Flow Meters?

A Positive Displacement Flow Meter is a device that measures liquid flow by capturing a known volume of fluid and counting the number of times that volume passes through the meter. Unlike flow meters that primarily measure flow velocity, a PD Flow Meter directly measures the quantity of liquid transferred. This makes it a useful choice for applications where accurate measurement of fuel, oil, solvents, and other liquids is important.

PD Flow Meters are particularly suitable for industries that handle intermittent flow, low flow rates, or liquids with varying viscosities. FluidTech's PD Flow Meter range includes models designed for precise measurement of liquid fuels and solvents, as well as industrial and pharmaceutical applications.
