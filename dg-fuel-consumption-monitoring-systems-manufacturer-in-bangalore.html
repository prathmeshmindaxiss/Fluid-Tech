What Is a DG Fuel Consumption Monitoring Systems?

A DG Fuel Consumption Monitoring System is a fuel measurement solution designed to determine how much diesel a generator actually consumes during operation.

The system measures fuel supplied to the DG and, where applicable, the fuel returned from the engine. By comparing these two measurements, it calculates the net fuel consumption. FluidTech's system uses an oval-gear positive displacement flow meter for diesel tracking and automatically calculates net consumption from supply and return measurements.

6. Fuel Management & Auditing
