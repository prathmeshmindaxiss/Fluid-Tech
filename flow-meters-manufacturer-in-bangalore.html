What Are Flow Meters?

A flow meter is an industrial measuring instrument used to determine the quantity or rate at which a liquid passes through a pipeline. Flow meters are widely used wherever precise liquid measurement is required for production, dispensing, batching, transfer, storage, or process monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Flow Meter?

A Flow Meter is an instrument used to measure the rate or quantity of liquid flowing through a pipeline. It is commonly used in industrial processes, liquid transfer, dispensing, and batching applications.

2. What is a Positive Displacement Flow Meter?

A Positive Displacement Flow Meter measures liquid by dividing the flowing liquid into defined volumes and counting those volumes. Fluid-Tech offers PD Flow Meters designed for accurate measurement of fuels, solvents, and other suitable liquids.
