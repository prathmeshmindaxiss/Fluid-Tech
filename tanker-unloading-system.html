Tanker Unloading System

Tanker Unloading System

The Fluid Tech Tanker Unloading System are designed to accurately measure and monitor the precise quantities of liquid fuels and solvents that are unloaded from tankers into underground storage tanks. It gives accurate and precise reading; it has high capacity air separator and control valve for assuring complete air elimination, The LCD with back light display ensures easy readability, the system comes with RS 485 output.

Core Specifications

  • Size: DN-50 (2") ANSI 150 Flanged
  • Flow Range: 1200 – 12000 LPH
  • Accuracy: ±0.25% of Reading
  • Pressure / Temp: 6 Kg/cm² Max / 80°C Max
  • Filtration: Inbuilt 149 Micron Steiner
  • Power Supply: AC 230 V 50 Hz

Advanced Components

  • Flameproof Display: Mounted LCD Backlight
  • Multi-Readout: Totalizer, Flow Rate & Hours
  • Air Separator: Built-in MS Fabricated & Coated
  • Premium Valves: Double Acting with S.S 316 Float
  • Output: Pulse Output Ready
